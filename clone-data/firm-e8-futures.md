Payout Policy

**E8 Signature Futures — Payout Criteria**

**35% Best Day rule:**

- No single trading day can exceed 35% of total profits.

**Minimum payout:**

- $100 (this is based on your payout share).
- With an 80% split, you must request at least $125.

**Minimum profitable days:**

- 5 profitable days between payouts.
- A profitable day = ≥ 0.3% realized (closed) PnL.
- After you request a payout, the profitable-day count resets to 0.

**Payout buffer required:**

- You must leave a buffer equal to your EOD Trailing Drawdown size (not withdrawable).

**Payout caps (max per payout request):**

- $25K: $1,000 / $1,000 / $1,250 / $1,250 / $1,500 (5+)
- $50K: $1,250 / $1,250 / $2,250 / $2,250 / $3,250 (5+)
- $100K: $2,250 / $2,250 / $3,250 / $3,250 / $4,250 (5+)

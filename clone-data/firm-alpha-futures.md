Firm Rules

**Minimum trading Days**

- Alpha Advance


Evaluation: 2 days \| Sim Funded: 5 days winning days of $200 or more
- Alpha Zero


Evaluation: 1 days \| Sim Funded: 5 days winning days of $200 or more
- Alpha Premium (Activation & No Activation Path)


Evaluation: 2 days \| Sim Funded: 5 days winning days of $200 or more

**News Trading Policy**

- Evaluation:
  - Allowed with No restrictions
- Sim Funded:
  - Advanced & Premium Qualified: Allowed with no restrictions
  - Zero Qualified: Positions can be held through news, but no trades may be executed within 2 minutes before or after high-impact news events (red folder events on ForexFactory). The 4-minute window applies to the start time of speeches only, not their full duration.
  - 1st violation: Warning only, account retained but profits on the trade voided and payout denied until next eligible date. Subsequent violations after warning will lead to breach of the account.

**Expert advisors**

- The use of AI, bots, and other automated trading mechanisms is strictly prohibited across all account types.
- Semi-automated trading (e.g. signal-based indicators) is allowed as long as the trader manually places, monitors and manages all trades.

**Copy Trading**

- Copy trading is permitted for a single user across their own accounts. Use your smallest account as the Master account to avoid complications. Alpha Futures is not liable for any issues arising from copy trading software.

**All or Nothing**

- All or nothing practices are prohibited.

**Average Trade Duration**

- Consistently just opening and closing positions less than 2 minutes to make profits without any plan.

**Gambling tendencies or "account rolling"**

- Taking max leverage positions with no plan, no stop loss, and hitting max loss on the account then buying more accounts and repeating the process until a trade hits.
- Tick or Micro Scalping (trades less than 10 ticks and less than 2 minutes)

**More on prohibited strategies**

- All or Nothing" Approach, Order Book Spamming, Tick or Micro Scalping, Exploiting Absence of Slippage, Simultaneous Multiple Limit Orders at Same Price, Gambling Tendencies / Account Rolling / Account Stacking, Reverse Trading / Hedging, Initiating Trades in Gapped or Illiquid Markets, Group Trading, VPN Usage to hide or change IP address
- See details: [https://help.alpha-futures.com/en/articles/9508585-prohibited-trading-practices](https://help.alpha-futures.com/en/articles/9508585-prohibited-trading-practices)

**Price Limit Trading**

- Traders are prohibited from trading any product within 2% of a CME price limit

**Drawdown Adjustment Rule**

- Withdrawing all profits after your trailing balance has been adjusted to the account balance will lead to account closure.

**Inactivity rule**

- Trading platforms archive trading data after 10 inactive trading days. To keep your account active, you must place a trade at least once every 10 trading days.

Payout Policy

**Premium (Activation & No Activation Path) Program**

- Profit split is 90% at all times. (not tiered)
- Weekly payouts after 5 winning days with $200+ profit each.
- You may request up to 50% of the profit (Up to the withdrawal limit) in each of your payout request, until you hit 30 trading days with $200 profit or more each day, then you may request up to 100% of the profit in your account - Note that withdrawing 100% down to the MLL permanently closes the account.
- Minimum payout is $500.

**Payout Cap per request, per account on all Premium account Sizes**

- 1st withdrawal $3,000 maximum
- 2nd withdrawal $3,500 maximum
- 3rd withdrawal $4,000 maximum
- 4th withdrawal $5,000 maximum
- 5th+ withdrawal $6,000 maximum

**Net Profit Rule**

- Accounts need to have a positive net profit (at least $1 ) more than the balance requested from previously.

* * *

**Zero Program**

- Profit split is 90% at all times (not tiered).
- Weekly payouts after 5 winning days with $200+ profit each and 40% consistency rule is met
- You may request up to 50% of the profit (Up to the withdrawal limit) in each of your payout request, until you hit 30 trading days with $200 profit or more each day, then you may request up to 100% of the profit in your account - Note that withdrawing 100% down to the MLL permanently closes the account.
- The minimum payout request on Zero Accounts is $200.
- Payout Cap per request, per account:
  - 25K Account - $1,000
  - 25K Account - $1,500
  - 25K Account - $2,500

* * *

**Advanced Program**

- Profit split is 90% at all times (not tiered).
- Weekly payouts after 5 winning days with $200+ profit each. Minimum payout is $1000, maximum is $15,000 on all Advanced Account sizes per request, per account.
- You may request up to 50% of the profit (Up to the withdrawal limit) in each of your payout request, until you hit 30 trading days with $200 profit or more each day, then you may request up to 100% of the profit in your account - Note that withdrawing 100% down to the MLL permanently closes the account.

_**The maximum payable balance on a simulated Qualified Account is +$40,000 per account, or 5 payouts. Once achieved, or if the Alpha Futures team decides to take action before these thresholds, the trader's history will be reviewed for the opportunity to move up to LIVE.**_

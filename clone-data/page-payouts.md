Additional Information

The Prop Firm Payouts Tracker shows tracked payout records across prop firms, giving traders a clearer way to compare payout performance using consistent metrics and filters.

On this page, you can compare total payouts, number of payouts, largest single payout, average payout size, and median payout time. You can also open any firm to view payout trends over time, recent payout records, and payout distribution by region.

A core goal of this tracker is transparency. By tracking payouts rather than relying on firm-submitted claims, the data provides a more objective view of payout activity. The Payouts Tracker is based on tracked payouts and updates automatically. For full context, use these payout metrics alongside firm rules and verified reviews.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the Prop Firm Payouts Tracker?

It's a dashboard that compares payouts across prop firms, including payout totals, payout count, payout size, and payout timing.

### Can I compare payout processing time across prop firms?

Yes. Use the same timeframe and market category, then sort by payout time.

### Does this page show payout rules or payout requirements?

No. It shows payout activity and timing. Eligibility depends on each firm's rules and review process.

### How often is the Payouts Tracker updated?

It updates as new payouts are tracked and processed.

### What does "median payout time" mean?

It's the typical payout waiting time. Median is less affected by unusually slow or unusually fast payouts.

### Why show both "average payout size" and "largest single payout"?

Average payout size shows what's typical. Largest single payout shows the biggest payout in the selected timeframe.

### How do I compare recent payouts vs long-term payout history?

Use the timeframe filter (last 7/30/365 days or all time).

### How do I keep comparisons consistent across firms?

Use the same timeframe and market category before comparing results.

### Do higher payout totals mean a firm is better?

Not necessarily. Totals can reflect scale and activity. Check payout count and payout time, and review the firm's rules.

### Are payout delays always a red flag?

Not necessarily. Delays can happen due to review windows, compliance checks, rule checks, payout methods, or higher volume periods.

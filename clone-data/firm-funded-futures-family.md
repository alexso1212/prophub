Firm Rules

**Minimum Trading Days**

- Prime - Evaluation: 1 day to Pass \| Sim Funded: 3 wining days of $200 or more each day
- Premier Plus (EOD & Intraday) - Evaluation: 1 day to Pass \| Sim Funded: 5 wining days of $200 or more each day
- Velocity - Evaluation: 3 days to pass \| Sim Funded:3 winning days of $200 or more each day. - Removed with Daily Payout Add-on
- S2F: 7 wining days of $200 or more each day

**Trading Day Definition**

- A “Trading Day” is defined as at least one trade placed between 6:00 PM EST/EDT and 5:00 PM EST/EDT the following day.

**News Trading**

- Allowed to trade through all news events across all account types, including Tier 1 events, as long as proper risk management is maintained
- News-based strategies designed to exploit volatility (e.g. straddles/strangles) and any attempt to disguise news-driven trades as regular trading are strictly prohibited.

**Expert Advisor**

- EAs and automated/algorithmic trading are not permitted — all trading must be manual and human-driven.

**Copy Trading**

- Manual copy trading allowed between your own FFF accounts
- Not allowed with trade copiers and between FFF accounts and third-party accounts (external signals, other traders or non-FFF accounts)

**Trading Hours**

- Trading is allowed anytime markets are open except between 4:15 PM – 6:00 PM EST; all trades are auto-closed at 4:15 PM EST if not manually closed.

**Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA)**

- DCA is permitted but must be used strategically — excessive averaging into losing positions to chase breakeven is discouraged.

**Microscalping Policy**

- Micro scalping is allowed but over 50% of trades and profits must come from positions held longer than 20 seconds.

**Inactivity Rule**

- At least one trade per week (with min hold time of 10 seconds) is required per account; accounts idle for 2 consecutive weeks are automatically closed and cannot be restored. Traders planning a break can contact support in advance to keep their account active.

**CME 2% Price Limit Rule**

- Trading is prohibited when a product is within 2% of its CME price limit on Sim Funded and Live Funded accounts. Failing to close a trade during a price limit extension results in account ineligibility. Traders are responsible for monitoring the % Net Change on their platform to avoid breaching this rule.

**Prohibited Practices**

- Martingale
- VPN Usage
- VPS Usage
- Automated Trading / EAs / Bots
- IP Manipulation
- Shared Account Access
- Multi-User Control
- Exceeding Maximum Accounts Per Household/IP Group
- Exploiting system discrepancies, delays, or technical issues, as well as using manipulative strategies like hedging to artificially game the system
- See details: [https://intercom.help/funded-futures-family/en/articles/11103767-fundedfuturesfamily-trading-environment-restrictions-faq](https://intercom.help/funded-futures-family/en/articles/11103767-fundedfuturesfamily-trading-environment-restrictions-faq)

Payout Policy

### **Profit Share**

- All Sim Funded Account payouts are subject to a 90/10 profit split.

* * *

### **Prime**

**Payout Frequency**:

- 3 winning days of $200 or more each day.

**Payout Profit Goals**

- To request payouts, traders must meet specific profit targets.
  - 25k Account: $300
  - 50k Account: $500
  - 100k Account: $750
  - 150k Account: $1,000
  - After each payout, the requirement resets and must be reached again

**Buffer Requirement**

- In order to request a payout, the account balance must remain above the required buffer level.

- **Buffer Formula:**


Drawdown + $100
  - 25k Account - Buffer Balance:$2,6100

  - 50k Account - Buffer Balance:$ 52,100

  - 100k Account - Buffer Balance:$103,100

  - 150k Account - Buffer Balance:$154,600

  - The buffer cannot be withdrawn and must always remain in the account.

**Payout Cap Per Request**

- 25k Account - 1st payout: $1,000 \| 2nd+ payout: $1,500
- 50k Account - 1st payout: $2,000 \| 2nd+ payout: $2,500
- 100k Account - 1st payout: $3,000 \| 2nd+ payout: $3,500
- 150k Account - 1st payout: $3,500 \| 2nd+ payout: $4,000

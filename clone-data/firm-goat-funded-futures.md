Firm Rules

**Minimum Trading Days**

**Instant**

- 7 winning days of 0.2% profit or more each day

**Pro**

- Evaluation: 2 days \| Sim Funded: 5 winning days of 0.2% profit or more each day

**EOD**

- Evaluation: 2 days \| Sim Funded: 7 winning days of 0.2% profit or more each day

**Sprint**

- Evaluation: 1 day \| Sim Funded: 5 winning days of 0.2% profit or more each day

**News restrictions**

- Challenge: No restrictions. Trade freely around news.
- Funded: 2-minute buffer around high-impact news. No opening, closing, or triggering pending orders within 2 minutes before or after a scheduled release.

**Expert Advisors**

- Personal automated strategies and EAs are allowed, as long as they are consistent with your trading style and do not exploit system vulnerabilities, latency, or data feed anomalies. Strategies designed to manipulate the simulated environment rather than reflect genuine market trading are prohibited.

**Copy Trading**

- Copy trading is allowed between accounts owned by the same person, including across different firms or brokers, provided ownership remains the same. Copy trading between different individuals is strictly prohibited.

**Micro scalping Rule**

- All Programs(Eval & Sim Funded) Except Pro Accounts


Profits from any trade (or partial close) closed within 2 minutes of opening will be deducted from the account. This applies to all account types except Pro and both Evaluation & Sim Funded. The trade itself is not a breach but the profits will not count toward balance or payout.

**All Sim Funded Daily Profit Cap**

- 5% of your account balance. Once this is reached, trading is automatically cut off for the day.

**Trading hours**

- Trading follows CME futures hours: Sunday 5:00 PM CT – Friday 4:00 PM CT, with a daily maintenance break from 4:00–5:00 PM CT. All positions must be closed before each session ends. Hours may vary by product — check CME's website for specifics.

**Prohibited Strategies**

- Micro scalping
- Latency exploitation
- High-frequency tick scalping
- Cross-account hedging
- Non-viable strategies
- If a strategy is found to be manipulating the simulated environment rather than reflecting genuine trading, it may result in account review or termination.

See details: [https://help.goatfundedfutures.com/en/articles/14111227-what-strategies-are-prohibited](https://help.goatfundedfutures.com/en/articles/14111227-what-strategies-are-prohibited)

**First Payout Profit Goals**

- 7% of the initial account Balance

Withdrawal Caps:

- 25k = 1k
- 50k = 2k
- 75k = 3k

- 150k = 4k

A new payout can only be requested after surpassing the equity high from before the previous withdrawal.

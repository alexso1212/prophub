Firm Rules

**2% Price Limit Rule**

- This is a restriction that applies when a futures instrument moves within 2% of the CME price limit.

**Prohibited Practices**

- **Account Rolling:** Account rolling is a high-risk practice where multiple Challenge accounts are purchased with intention to quick succession of funded phase, with some accounts being intentionally sacrificed while focusing on passing others.

- **Multi-Order Spam and Coordinated Trading Exploits:** Multi-Order Spam and Coordinated Trading Exploits involve placing and canceling numerous orders in the Depth of Market (DOM) to manipulate market activity or collaborating with others to artificially influence order flow.

- **Micro scalping:** Micro scalping is defined as the practice of opening and closing trades within seconds to capture very small, quick price changes. While scalping itself is a valid trading approach, at FundedNext, micro-scalping is identified when 40% or more of the total profit is generated from trades that are closed within 10 seconds.

- **Bracket Strategies:** Bracket trading is a strategy where traders set predefined buy & sell (limit/stop orders) to take advantage of any sided market movement and also set profit-taking and stop-loss levels to manage risk and lock in gains automatically.

- **Account Flipping and Inconsistent Contract Execution:** Account Flipping and Inconsistent Contract Execution involve using high margin to rapidly grow or blow-up accounts through reckless, oversized positions.

- Find more prohibited practices here: [https://helpfutures.fundednext.com/en/articles/10800231-prohibited-trading-strategy-of-fundednext-futures](https://helpfutures.fundednext.com/en/articles/10800231-prohibited-trading-strategy-of-fundednext-futures)


**Maximum Performance Reward**

- Withdrawing 100% causes a Hard Breach due to Maximum Loss Limit reset at the starting balance.

**Inactivity Rule**

- Challenge Accounts: Marked as breached and deactivated after 7 consecutive calendar days of no trades.
- FundedNext Accounts: Marked as inactive and deactivated after 30 consecutive calendar days of no trades.

Payout Policy

**Rapid**

- Benchmark Days: No benchmark days required.

- ​Consistency Rule: Daily profit cannot exceed 40% of total profits in the account.


​Cycle in Profit:

- ​First withdrawal requires at least $250 (25K/50K accounts) or $500 (100K accounts).

- For subsequent withdrawals, the same minimum amounts apply ($250 or $500 depending on account size).

- ​Minimum Withdrawal Amounts:
  - $250 for 25K and 50K accounts
  - $500 for 100K accounts
- Maximum Withdrawal per Cycle:
  - ​$800 (25K)
  - $1,500 (50K)
  - $2,500 (100K)
- Cap Removal Threshold:
  - After 5 Rewards, the cap is lifted, and traders can withdraw their full profits without limits.
- Reward Share:
  - Traders receive 80% of the requested Performance Reward.


    ​Growth Calculations: Based on the withdrawn amount.

​Important Notes:

- Withdrawing 100% of your gain in one request causes a hard breach.
- After your first withdrawal, the maximum loss limit (MLL) resets to the initial balance.

* * *

**Legacy**

- Benchmark Days: Minimum 5 benchmark days before the first withdrawal.

- Benchmark profits: $100 (25K), $200 (50K), $200 (100K).


​\- Cycle in Profit: At least $500 profit is required after your last withdrawal before requesting another.

- ​Minimum Withdrawal Amount: $250.

- ​Maximum Withdrawal (before 30 Benchmark Days):
  - You can withdraw a maximum of $3,000, $6,000, or $6,000, depending on the account size ($25K, $50K, $100K).
  - Traders may withdraw up to 50% of their profits until they complete 30 benchmark days.
- After 30 Benchmark Days: Traders can withdraw 100% of their profits.

- ​Reward Share: Traders receive 80% of the requested Performance Reward.

- ​Growth Calculations: Based on the withdrawn amount.


​Important Notes:

- The first Performance Reward resets the MLL back to the initial balance.
- Withdrawing 100% of your gain in one go causes a hard breach.

* * *

**Bolt**

- Withdrawable Amount: Profit above $52,100
- Minimum Withdrawal: $250
- Maximum Withdrawal per Payout: $1,200
- Profit Split: Traders receive 80% of the requested Performance Reward.
- Maximum Loss Limit After First Withdrawal: The maximum loss limit locks at $50,100.
- Withdrawal Cap: Maximum Withdrawal Allowed: 5
- After the 4th withdrawal, the FundedNext (FN) Account proceeds to its final settlement stage. The outcome depends on the End-of-Day (EOD) account balance.
- Final settlement (5th payout): $7,700. Any simulated profit above $7,700 is forfeited.

* * *

**Flex**

- Benchmark Days: Minimum 5 benchmark days before the first withdrawal.
- Benchmark profits: $200 (50K), $200 (100K), $250 (150K).
- ​Cycle in Profit: At least $500 profit is required after your last withdrawal before requesting another.
- ​Minimum Withdrawal Amount: $250.
- Maximum Withdrawal: You can withdraw a maximum up to 50% up to a maximum cap depending on account size after every 5 benchmark days ($50K - $1500, $100K - $2000, $150K - $4000).​
- Reward Share: Traders receive 80% of the requested Performance Reward.
- The first Performance Reward resets the MLL back to the initial balance.
- Withdrawing 100% of your gain in one go causes a hard breach.

* * *

**Live Funded**

- Eligibility: Eligible for review after $100,000 in Total Active Profits across all active FundedNext Futures accounts (withdrawals + current simulated profits).
- Eligible Profit = 80% of Total Simulated Profit and is split into:
- 50% Settlement Withdrawal
- 25% Live Trading Deposit (capped at $50,000)
- Reserve = remaining amount (no cap)

**Maximum Loss Limit (Auto Liquidation Threshold)**

- Static equity limit (includes unrealized losses). If equity falls below it, the live account is liquidated and permanently closed.

**Withdrawal Policy**

- Before first withdrawal: threshold = 20% of initial Live Deposit
- After first withdrawal: locks at initial Live Deposit and never trails/increases
- Reserve System (auto-refills)
- First $10,000 withdrawn: 100% match from Reserve
- After $10,000: 50% match from Reserve
- Refills stop when Reserve reaches $0.

**Profit Split**

- 80% (on withdrawals)

**Withdrawals**

- Minimum: $100
- Must generate $500 in new closed profit between withdrawals (after the first)


Full balance withdrawal: after 45 Benchmark days (each day $150+ profit), leaving $160 in the account; closes the Live account and forfeits remaining Reserve

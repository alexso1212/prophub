Additional Information

The Trader Payouts Leaderboard showcases the top traders ranked by payouts reported by leading prop firms. It offers a transparent view of trading performance and is shared directly by firms, allowing traders to see how [payouts](https://propfirmmatch.com/futures/payouts) are distributed across the industry.

On this page, you can see the respective prop firm, payout amount, account size, payout method, and the region of the trader.

The Trader Payouts Leaderboard gives traders a look at what can be achieved within funded accounts. For experienced traders, it offers a point of reference when assessing firms and their payout practices. The goal is not to promote individual firms or traders, but to provide an accessible, data-driven overview of firm-payouts across the prop trading landscape.

At [Prop Firm Match](https://propfirmmatch.com/futures), our aim is to bring more transparency to the prop trading space. By gathering and presenting payout information directly from firms, the Trader Leaderboard gives traders a better perspective on real trader performance across the industry.

Explore the leaderboard to see the top firm-reported payouts.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the Payouts Trader Leaderboard?

The leaderboard shows the largest trader payouts reported by participating prop firms.

### How are payouts ranked?

They are ranked by size, with the biggest payouts appearing at the top.

### How often is the Trader Payouts Leaderboard updated?

It updates whenever firms provide new payout data.

### What time period does the leaderboard cover?

You can filter results by last 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, 365 days, or all time.

### Who is included on the leaderboard?

Only traders from firms that share payout data are shown.

### Why isn't my name or payout showing up?

Your payout may not have been reported yet, may still be processing, or your firm may not share data with Prop Firm Match.

### Is this data public? Can others see my payouts?

Yes, all payouts shown are public, but only the details firms provide are displayed.

### What should I do if I notice an error in my payout?

Contact your prop firm directly. Prop Firm Match shows data as it is reported and cannot change it.

### Where can I read verified reviews of prop firms?

You can find [Verified Prop Firm Reviews from Real Traders](https://propfirmmatch.com/prop-firm-reviews) on Prop Firm Match. These reviews are written by actual traders to help you make informed decisions.

### Who can I contact for support or questions?

For leaderboard questions, reach us through our [Contact Page](https://propfirmmatch.com/contact). For payout issues, contact your prop firm.

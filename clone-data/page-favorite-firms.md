Additional Information

The Favorite Firms page shows how traders are choosing to support the firms they trust most. Each firm's favorite count reflects real user selections, giving a clear snapshot of community preference over time.

On this page, you can browse firms, search by name, and compare how many traders have selected each firm as their favorite. You can also set or change your own favorite at any time.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What does the Favorite Firms page show?

The Favorite Firms page displays a ranked list of prop firms based on how many traders have selected each one as their favorite. It gives you a real-time snapshot of community preference across all verified firms.

### How do I set or change my favorite firm?

Sign in to your Prop Firm Match account, find the firm you want to support, and click the favorite button. You can change your selection at any time — your previous choice will be replaced automatically.

### Can I only favorite one firm at a time?

Yes, each trader can select one favorite firm at a time. This keeps the ranking meaningful and ensures every vote reflects a genuine current preference.

### How often is the favorite count updated?

Favorite counts update in real time. As soon as a trader sets or changes their favorite, the totals on this page reflect the new count.

### Can I search for a specific firm on this page?

Yes, use the search bar at the top of the favorites table to filter firms by name. The list updates instantly as you type.

### Are all firms on this page verified by Prop Firm Match?

Yes, only firms that have passed our strict compliance and vetting process appear on this page. The favorite count reflects community preference among verified firms only.

Firm Rules

**Inactivity rule**

- A trade must be placed at least once within each seven-day period. Failing to trade within this timeframe may result in your account being marked inactive and breached.

**Prohibited Activities**

Here are the types of trading you cannot engage in:

**Automated Scalping**

- The use of automated systems designed for ultra-high-frequency scalping, particularly systems that exceed 200 trades per day is prohibited.

**Fully Automated Trading Systems**

- You must be actively involved in trading and cannot let software do all the work for you.

**Market Manipulation**

- This includes trying to artificially affect the market price by placing multiple orders or other dishonest strategies.

**Micro scalping**

- This is a strategy where trades are held for very brief moments to capture tiny price movements. It's not allowed.

**Copy Trading**

- Copy trading is allowed if the account you copy from belongs to you, but it cannot be used across multiple evaluation accounts.

**2%price limit rule**

- Aquafutures prohibits users from trading within 2 % of a CME price limit. A price limit refers to the maximum amount a futures contract is allowed to move during a trading session. If the limit is reached, the market may pause or halt depending on the specific product and the time of day.

**Biggest Loss vs. Biggest Win (single trade)**

- Your biggest single trade loss cannot exceed your biggest single trade win during the funded stage within each withdrawal cycle. The calculation is applied to net profits (commissions included).

Payout Policy

**Rewards, Withdrawals & Profit Split Rules**

**Profit Split**

- 100% profit split on withdrawals up to a lifetime total of $15,000 per user
- 90% profit split applies once the $15,000 lifetime threshold is exceeded
- The lifetime threshold applies per user, across all accounts, profiles, and email addresses.

**Reward Eligibility**

**Beginner Accounts**

- Reward Frequency: Every 7 days

**Requirements:**
- 5 winning days with a minimum of $100/ $200 / $360 / $580 depending on account size
- 40% consistency rule
- Minimum Reward: $250
- Biggest single trade loss must not exceed biggest single trade win (each entry/exit is treated as its own trade)

**Profit Requirement (beginner accounts)**

- First cycle: 7% profits

- Second cycle: 6% profits

- Third and all future cycles: 5% profits


**Standard Accounts**

- Reward Frequency: On-Demand

**Requirements:**
- 5 winning days with a minimum of $120 / $200 / $360 / $580 depending on account size
- 40% consistency rule
- Minimum Reward: $500
- Biggest single trade loss must not exceed biggest single trade win (each entry/exit is treated as its own trade)

**Buffer Zone Policy (Standard Accounts)**

Buffer Zone Amount:

- $25K: $26,100

- $50K: $52,100

- $100K: $103,100

- $150k: $154,600

- When requesting within the buffer zone, you are required to withdraw 60% of your profits, the remaining 40% will stay in your account.


**Instant Funded & Instant Funded Pro Accounts**

- Reward Frequency: On demand (when eligible)
- Requirements:

1. 7 winning days
2. Minimum daily profit based on account size ($100 / $200)
3. 20% consistency for both
4. Minimum Reward: $250
5. Biggest single trade loss must not exceed biggest single trade win (each entry/exit is treated as its own trade)

**Profit requirement applies per reward cycle:**

- Cycle 1: 6%
- Cycle 2: 5%
- Cycle 3 and onward: 4%

**Drawdown Adjustment**

- Upon requesting your first reward, max drawdown adjusts to starting balance + $100
- Reward requirements must be met within each reward cycle separately

**Buffer Zone Policy (Standard Accounts Only)**

- $25K → $26,300
- $50K → $52,600
- $100K → $103,600
- $150K → $155,100

**Withdrawals Within the Buffer Zone**

- Required withdrawal: 60% of profits
- Remaining 40% stays in the account
- Minimum withdrawal: $500

**Withdrawals Below the Buffer Zone**

- Max drawdown resets to starting balance + $100
- Trading continues without a trailing drawdown

**Maximum Reward Cap**

- Applies during the first 60 days on Beginner and Standard accounts, cap is lifted after 60 days of trading and at least 3 successful rewards

**Maximum Reward cap (All except Standard Accounts)**

- $25,000 Account: $750 per request

- $50,000 Account: $1,500 per request

- $100,000 Account: $3,000 per request

- $150,000 Account: $4,500 per request

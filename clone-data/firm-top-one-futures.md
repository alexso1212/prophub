Firm Rules

**Overnight Holding**

- Overnight trading is NOT allowed. All positions must be closed by 4:10 PM EST each trading day.

- Any open trades after this time may trigger forced liquidation and lead to potential account penalties.


**News Trading**

- Elite, Instant Sim Funded, Ignite Instant Funding and Elite Access evaluation phase: Allowed without any restrictions.
- Elite Access Funded phase: Not allowed 2 minutes before and after high impact news.

**Expert Advisor & Bot**

- The use of Expert Advisors (EAs) or bots is not allowed. The firm requires all traders to engage in manual, skill-based trading to ensure fairness and integrity in the trading environment.

**Copy Trading**

- Copy trading from external sources is strictly prohibited.

- Top One Futures allows copy trading/mirroring to other firms only if trades originate from your own activity and discretion on their platform; signal following, group trading, or mirroring others' trades is strictly prohibited.

- Copy trading is allowed only between accounts of the same type and size within Top One Futures (e.g., Elite $50K → Elite $50K, Instant $150K → Instant $150K, Ignite 100K → Ignite 100K)

- A maximum of 3 Elite Sim or Instant Sim accounts can be linked for copy trading.

- A maximum of 6 IGNITE Instant Funding accounts can be used for copy trading.


**Disclaimer – Instant Sim Funded Accounts:**

- If you already have 3 × $150K Instant Sim Funded Accounts, you cannot add 2 × $50K (or any other mix).
- If you have 3 × $50K, you can now add 2 more.
- If you have any Elite Funded Accounts, the cap remains a total of 3 Elite/Instant Sim Funded accounts.

**Minimum Trade Duration (10-Second Rule)**

- To protect against ultra-high-frequency scalping and promote responsible trading:

- All trades must remain open longer than 10 seconds.

- Occasional minor violations are tolerable, but if: anywhere close to 50% or more of your total and/or withdrawable profits OR, anywhere close to 50% or more of your individual trades violate this rule, all profits associated with these trades will be removed.


**Trading Hours**

- 6:00 PM EST and 4:10 PM EST the next day, Monday through Friday.

- There is a daily 1-hour break from 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM EST, during which trading is paused. After the break, the next session opens.


**Inactivity Rule**

- All Sim Funded traders must place at least one trade every 14 calendar days to keep their account in good standing.

- Note: There is no inactivity timer on the Elite challenge phase.


**Prohibited Trading Practices**

- Top One Futures prioritizes the safety, sustainability, and integrity of their platform, ensuring a long-term, secure, and fair-trading environment for everyone.

- All prohibited practices can be found via this link:

[https://intercom.help/top-one-futures/en/articles/11021584-prohibited-trading-practices](https://intercom.help/top-one-futures/en/articles/11021584-prohibited-trading-practices)

### Payout Cycle

- **Profit Split**: 90/10% profit split

- **Profit Target:**
  - 1st payout: 6% of initial balance
  - 2nd payout: 5%
  - 3rd payout: 4%
- **Minimum payout:** 2%

- **Maximum Payout:**
  - $25,000 Account: Max $1,500 per withdrawal

  - $50,000 Account: Max $2,500 per withdrawal

  - $100,000 Account: Max $3,000 per withdrawal

  - $150,000 Account: Max $3,500 per withdrawal

  - $25,000 Account: $200

  - $150,000 Account: $350
- **50% Profit Progression Rule**
  - For each payout request, at least 50% of the requested payout amount must come from profits generated since your last payout.

### Instant

- **Profit Split**: 90/10% profit split

- **The Profit Target (Dynamic)**
  - First Payout: You must make 6% of your initial account balance.

  - Second Payout: You must make 5% on top of your new balance (after the first payout is deducted).

  - Third Payout (and all future payouts): You must make 4% on top of your new balance (after the previous payout is deducted).
- **Maximum payout amount:**
  - $25,000 Account: Max $1,500 per withdrawal

  - $50,000 Account: Max $2,500 per withdrawal

  - $100,000 Account: Max $3,000 per withdrawal

  - $150,000 Account: Max $3,500 per withdrawal

  - Available once you have achieved a 5% profit and met the 15% consistency rule.

  - No minimum trading day requirement.
- **Subsequent Payouts**
  - Each payout requires another 5% profit relative to the balance after the last payout.
- Subsequent Payouts: Each payout requires a new 5% profit based on your balance after the last payout.

- **Minimum Payout**:
  - $250

  - 25K = $500

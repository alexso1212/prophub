Firm Rules

**Inactivity period**

- 7 consecutive calendar days (Sunday–Friday) - A trading day is only counted when at least one round-trip trade is fully completed

**News Trading**

- News trading is permitted across all impact levels but is not recommended due to risks of price gaps, volatility, and slippage.

**Copy Trading**

- Copy trading is permitted between your own accounts under one user profile, including challenge-to-challenge and sim funded-to- sim funded( same environment). The Lead account must have the lowest maximum position size among all linked accounts. Third-party copiers are allowed but any issues must be resolved directly with the provider. Copying between accounts owned by different individuals is not permitted.

**Expert Advisors**

- Prohibited to employ any kind of semi or fully automated trading like EAs, Bots, AI, etc. on all accounts

**Risk Limits and Stop Loss Rules**

**Risk Per Trade Idea**

- Maximum risk per trade idea is 1% of initial balance (DX Futures) and 2% of initial balance (Tradovate & NinjaTrader) on sim funded and Direct accounts. This includes;
- Single Trade Idea
- Layering/Splitting Positions - multiple positions on the same assets, same direction, with overlapping time are collectively treated as one single trade idea.
- The 10-Minute Re-entry Rule - Opening a new position in the same direction on the same asset within 10 minutes of closing counts as the continuation of the same trade idea.
- Breach of this rule will result in a hard breach and termination of the account.

**Stop Loss Rule**

- A stop loss must be placed within 3 minutes of opening a position or prior to closing the position, whichever occurs earlier to define this risk limits

**Trading Hours Explained**

- [https://holaprime.com/futures/faq/hola-prime-plans/hola-prime-1-step-prime-challenge/](https://holaprime.com/futures/faq/hola-prime-plans/hola-prime-1-step-prime-challenge/)

**Circuit Breaker Limits**

- Traders may NOT place trades within 2% of the price limit. Doing so will result in account closure. Please refer to CME Price Limits to check specific price limits for different asset classes.

**Prohibited Strategies**

- Gambling / Speculative Trading, Hyperactivity / Excessive Trading, Account or Device Sharing, One-Sided Betting, Arbitrage / Tick Scalping / High-Frequency Trading (HFT), Copy Trading Across Different Users, Hedging between different accounts/ Reverse Trades, Gap Trading, Trading Without Technical or Fundamentals, Trading Behavior Inconsistency, Circuit Breaker Limit Violations, Non-Compliance with CME Group Rules.

- See details: [https://holaprime.com/futures/prohibited-trading-practices/](https://holaprime.com/futures/prohibited-trading-practices/)

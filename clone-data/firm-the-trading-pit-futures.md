Firm Rules

**Copy-Trading**

- Traders are allowed to copy trades manually or automatically in up to 5 of their accounts.
- Copying trades between Challenge & Earning accounts is prohibited.
- Traders are allowed to copy trades from a personal external account to TTP accounts.
- Copying trades from other traders or sources that are not your own is prohibited.

**Expert Advisor (EA)**

- Allowed to trade using your own Expert Advisor (EA), as long as it does not perform any prohibited trading activities outlined in the terms and conditions.

**News Trading**

- **Futures Prime:** News trading is allowed.

**Prohibited Trading Strategies**

- Martingale or similar high-risk trading strategies are not allowed.

- Grid trading is not allowed.

- Gap trading is not allowed.

- Overnight holding rule: It is not allowed to keep positions open overnight in Futures Prime accounts

- High-Frequency Trading (HFT) is not permitted on any of our accounts.


**Scalping**

- Scalping is allowed in all futures accounts.

**Inactivity**

- If no trade is placed for 21 consecutive days, the account will be considered inactive and will be suspended.

**Gambling and Inconsistent Trading**

- Traders must maintain consistent lot sizes in their trades, demonstrating disciplined and strategic trading practices.

Payout Policy

**Futures Prime:** Traders will be paid an 80% profit share.

**First Reward**

- Maximum of $5,000 USD or 50% of realized profits, whichever is lower
- Requires five (5) winning trading days, with at least $200 profit per day
- The five (5) winning days do not need to be consecutive

**Second Reward and Onwards**

- Maximum of $5,000 USD or 50% of realized profits, whichever is lower
- Requires five (5) winning trading days, with at least $200 profit per day
- The five (5) winning days do not need to be consecutive
- A profit greater than $0 since the last reward is required
- Any positive profit qualifies (even $0.01)

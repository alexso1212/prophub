Firm Rules

**News Trading**

- Not Allowed: All positions are auto-closed 2 minutes before Tier 1 news releases and markets reopen 2 minutes after. No account failure for accidental news trades. Traders can view the Tier 1 data release calendar, the Investing.com Economic Calendar, and the CME Calendar in the TradeDay Members area.

**Copy Trading Rule**

- Copy trading is permitted across your own evaluation and funded sim accounts. Trades can also be copied from a personal external account into your TradeDay account. Funded Live and Funded Sim accounts must be traded separately, copy trading between them is not permitted. Copy trading between 2 or 3 Funded Sim accounts only is allowed.

**Consistency Rule**

- Profits during one single trading day should not exceed 30% of the profit target. There is no consistency objective for funded traders.

**Minimum of 5 Days Objective**

- Minimum of 5 days of trading in order to complete this objective. No need to be consecutive. Traders finishing the profit target and the consistency objectives before completing 5 days are required to continue to trade in the style, they have been. Microtrading is not allowed.

**Expert Advisors**

- Expert advisors are allowed; however, algos and bots purchased from a third party and employed by the trader are forbidden. If multiple users are making the same trades, then all the accounts will be shut down and the users suspended.

**Gap Trading**

- Entering trades specifically in gapped, illiquid markets to benefit from isolated/stray fills are not allowed.

**Hedging prohibited**

- Simultaneously going long in one account and short in another account in the same product.

**VPN Usage**

- Using a VPN or VPS to mask the location of trading is not allowed.

**High Frequency Trading (HFT)**

- Using automated trading systems designed to scalp at an extremely high frequency is prohibited.
- Strategies that produce more than 200 trades in a day is prohibited.
- Not allowed to use any trading strategy intended to exploit or create errors in the services, such as errors in the display of prices or delays in their updates.
- Traders can only have one Funded Live account. When in Funded Live, you will not be able to purchase additional accounts.
- Trading an excessive or unrealistic number of contracts in the day is not allowed.

**Exploiting System Errors**

- Not allowed to use any trading strategy intended to exploit or create errors in the services, such as errors in the display of prices or delays in their updates.

**Inactivity Rule**

- Accounts inactive for a period of 30 days without prior consent are considered dormant and will be closed.

**Circuit Breaker Rule**

- Funded Traders are not permitted to trade within 2% of a price limit. Traders in breach of this policy will lose their funded account.

Payout Policy

- Traders cannot make a withdrawal request until they have cleared the buffer.

- Traders can draw down into the buffer when they have cleared the buffer
- A trader clears the Buffer Zone when they have a positive balance at the equivalent of their Drawdown.
- Minimum payout request is $250

- Traders can withdraw profits whenever they like, and there are no restrictions on the frequency of withdrawals.

**TradeDay offers profit split of:**

- 80% profit share on the first $50K of lifetime withdrawals

- 90% profit share from $50K to $100K

- 95% profit share beyond $100K

- Buffer zone funds are available anytime but are subject to a 50% profit share


**Balance required for payout (Buffer)**

- **EOD and Intraday**
  - 50K: $2,000
  - 100K: $3,000
  - 150K: $4,000
- **Static**
  - 50K: $500
  - 100K: $750
  - 150K: $1,000

**EOD and Intraday Drawdown Lock**

- Once the buffer is cleared, the Trailing Max Drawdown trails up and locks at the starting balance.

**Funded Sim Milestones**

- Funded Sim milestones are profit checkpoints set at every $5,000 up to $20,000 ($5K, $10K, $15K, $20K). At each milestone, trading is paused and the Head of Trading reviews the account to decide whether to extend the trader in Funded Sim or move them to a Funded Live account. Milestones do not affect payout eligibility; withdrawals can be requested at any time once the buffer is cleared. Most traders reaching $20,000 are moved to Funded Live.

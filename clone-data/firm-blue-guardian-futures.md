Firm Rules

**Trading Without Stops:**

- Trading without stop-loss strategies and relying on the Trailing Threshold to close a trade as a means of ""blowing the account"" is not allowed.

**Automated Tools:**

- AI, bots, and other fully automated trading mechanisms are strictly prohibited across all account types.

**All-in Trading:**

- Using maximum contract sizes to chase a ""big win"" early in the Funded Account evaluation process, with the hope of overcoming the trailing drawdown, then downsizing later is prohibited.

**Price Limit Trading:**

- Traders are prohibited from trading any product within 2% of a CME price limit

**News Trading:**

- News trading is not allowed 5 minutes before and after a red folder data release.

**Inactivity Rule (Funded stage):**

- To keep your Funded Account active, you must place at least one trade within each seven-day period.

**Risk to Reward Ratio's:**

- Traders are to adhere to a maximum risk-to-reward ratio of 5:1.

**Inconsistent Sizing & Martingale Strategies:**

- Trading strategies that involve inconsistent contract sizing, especially those that increase risk exposure in an attempt to recover losses commonly referred to as ""martingale"" strategies are prohibited.

**Order Placement:**

- Placing multiple limit orders at the same price to manipulate order fills is strictly prohibited.
- Micro scalping is prohibited

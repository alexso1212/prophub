Firm Rules

**EA/Bots/Algorithmic Trading**

- Algorithmic trading is allowed provided you exclusively own the strategy, it is not shared with other traders or firms, it does not involve high-frequency trading, and ownership can be verified upon request.

**Microscalping**

- Microscalping refers to executing trades to capture very small price movements within a very short timeframe, and over 50% of both trades and profits must come from positions held longer than 10 seconds.

**Maximum Account Idle Time (Funded Accounts)**

- To keep your funded account active, you must place at least one trade per week (Monday through Friday). This is per account, not per user.
- Failure to do so may result in your account being marked as inactive.

**Copy trading**

- You may only group trade between accounts you own and manage. Copying other strategies or engaging in copy trading with others is not permitted.
- If you choose to use third-party trade copiers, you do so at your own risk. We are not responsible for any errors they may cause, and support for such issues is beyond our scope.

**News Trading, Dollar Cost Averaging, Flipping, and Scaling**

- We do not have any rules against or guidelines around trading news events, Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA), flipping, or scaling.

- NEVER use the Daily Loss Limit as a stop loss - System protection may not be effective during extreme volatility

- Using the DLL as a stop may cause your account to breach the Trailing Max Drawdown due to slippage in volatile markets.

- Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA) refers to entering a trade with multiple executions at different price points from the initial entry.

- DCA is allowed, but it should always be part of a structured strategy. It is discouraged to “average into oblivion” involving continuously adding to a losing position without a clear strategy, until the trade reaches breakeven or a slight profit.


**Hedging & Trading Micros or Minis Rules**

- Hedging is 100% prohibited for all accounts and its detected automatically.
- Traders are not permitted to trade micro and mini futures contracts simultaneously.
- Switching between them across different sessions is allowed, but you cannot open or hold both types of positions at once.


More on hedging rule: [https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/10495868-rules-hedging-trading-micros-or-minis](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/10495868-rules-hedging-trading-micros-or-minis)

**Simulated Funded Accounts:**

- An individual user may have up to 5 Simulated Funded Accounts at any time
- A household is also restricted to 5 Simulated Funded Accounts at any time

Other trading rules: [https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/12268167-essential-trading-rules-overview](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/12268167-essential-trading-rules-overview)

- 25K Account: 50% of account balance up to $1,250

- 100K Account: Up to 50% of balance with a cap of $4,000 per payout

**No Minimum Balance Requirement**

- Drawdown locking is not linked to payouts and occurs automatically when EOD balance exceeds the drawdown threshold by $100 (e.g., $52,100 on a $50K account).This happens automatically regardless of whether you've requested a payout.,

- For subsequent payouts (2nd and beyond), you must show positive net profit during the payout cycle (plus meet any Trading Days with Profit requirement) to be eligible


**Buffer System**

- The Daily Payout Policy uses a buffer amount that must be exceeded before you can request payouts:

- 25K Account: $1,100 buffer

- 50K Account: $2,100 buffer

- 100K Account: $2,600 buffer

- 150K Account: $3,600 buffer


You cannot withdraw profits that would bring your account balance below the buffer amount. Profits above the buffer are eligible for withdrawal, up to the payout cap.

**Payout Caps**

To be eligible for a payout on any day:

- The trader must have profit greater than $0 since their last payout, and they may request up to 2× the profit earned above the buffer, capped at:

- $600 for a 25K account

- $1,000 for a 50K account

- $1,500 for a 100K account

- $2,500 for a 150K account


This cap resets after each payout cycle.

Minimum payout: $250

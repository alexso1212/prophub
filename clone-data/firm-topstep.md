Firm Rules

**Minimum Trading Days**

- Evaluation (Trading Combine): 2 Days (tied to the 50% consistency score)
- Funded (Express Funded Standard Path): 5 winning days of $150+
- Express Funded (Consistency Path): 3 days with 40% consistency target

**News Trading**

- Allowed with no restrictions during Evaluation and Funded stages.

**Copy Trading**

- Trade copying can only be applied to accounts within the same trading environment. you can copy trades from a trading combine to an Express Funded Account, and from an Express from a trading combine to an Express Funded Account, and from an Express Funded Account to a Trading combine. Live Funded Accounts cannot utilize the Trade Copier.

**Automated strategies and EA's**

- Automated strategies (EAs) are allowed, but traders are fully responsible for their setup and performance. Bots must comply with all trading rules no manipulative or unfair software and TopstepX only supports automation via API access.‍

**On Express Funded account‍**

- After payout the capital remaining in your account will be the maximum amount you will be able to lose. Your maximum loss limit will always be set to $0 after your payout.

**Inactivity**

- Express Funded Account must have at least one trade every 30 days to remain open.

**Prohibited Trading Strategies**

- Account Stacking
- Intentional Account Depletion
- Terms Violation
- Use of Manipulative Tools (AI or Automation)
- Abnormal Market Behavior
- Trading Outside Market Range
- News Event Exploitation

**More Prohibited trading conduct can be found via this link:**

- [https://intercom.help/topstep-llc/en/articles/10296582-prohibited-conduct](https://intercom.help/topstep-llc/en/articles/10296582-prohibited-conduct)

**VPN Rule**

- The use of VPNs, Proxy Services, TOR, geo-location obfuscation, and other potential identity masking services are not permitted

**Trading Combine/Reset Purchase Limits**

- Traders can purchase up to 20 new Trading Combine subscriptions per month.
- Traders are allowed 2 Resets per account per calendar day.

**The Do's and Don'ts of SIM Fills**

- Topstep prohibits any attempts to exploit or manipulate the simulated environment and may reject profits if abuse is detected.
- Practices like scalping algorithms, rapid-fire trades, impossible fills, or unrealistic stop-loss execution are not allowed.
- The rule targets intentional abusive behavior, not normal trading and exists to ensure traders focus on real, consistent skill development.
- See details: [https://help.topstep.com/en/articles/10431370-the-do-s-and-don-ts-of-sim-fills](https://help.topstep.com/en/articles/10431370-the-do-s-and-don-ts-of-sim-fills)

**2% Price Limit Rule**

- Topstep do not allow market participation when a product is trading within 2% of a Price Limit. Applies to all levels of Topstep's program.

Payout Policy

**Topstep's Profit Split** \- 90%

**Express Funded Standard Path**

- 5 winning days of $150+
- Can request 50% of account balance up to $5,000
- Achieve a profit greater than $0 since your last payout

**Express Funded Consistency Path**

- 3 Days with 40% consistency target
- Can request 50% of account balance up to $6,000
- Achieve a profit greater than $0 since your last payout

**Payout Cap**

**Express Funded Account Standard**

- 50k - $2000
- 100k - $3000
- 150k - $5000

**Express Funded Account Consistency**

- 50k - $3000
- 100k - $4000
- 150k - $6000

**Minimum Payout Amount**

- $125

**Payout Methods**

- Wise: 1-3 business days

- ACH: 1-3 business days

- Wire/SWIFT: 3-5 business days

- International Wire/SWIFT: up to 5-10 business days​

- $30 processing fee with ACH or Wire


Once the payout request is processed, the Maximum Loss Limit will automatically be set to $0 (the trader cannot allow the account balance to reach or go below $0 after a payout).

**More details can be found via this link:**

[https://intercom.help/topstep-llc/en/articles/8284233-topstep-payout-policy](https://intercom.help/topstep-llc/en/articles/8284233-topstep-payout-policy)

**Scaling Plan**

- The Scaling Plan is an objective in the Express Funded Account. It's evaluated each day when your Trade Report is updated.
- See details: [https://intercom.help/topstep-llc/en/articles/8284223-what-is-the-scaling-plan](https://intercom.help/topstep-llc/en/articles/8284223-what-is-the-scaling-plan)?

**Live Funded Account:**

- Traders can request a payout of up to 50% of their account balance after accumulating five winning trading days per payout request. A winning trading day is counted when a day’s Net PNL is $150 or more.
- Traders can request Daily payouts and access 100% of the profits after accumulating 30 winning trading days (After which the Live Funded Account will be closed since the balance will be brought to the Maximum Loss Limit.)

**Live Funded**

- Traders can have only one Live account at a time ($50K / $100K / $150K)

- Tier is based on the average size of payout-eligible Express Funded Accounts (XFAs)

- 20% of balance is tradable at activation on live funded accounts

- Remaining balance unlocks in 20% increments through performance milestones


**Daily loss limits scale by tier**

- $50K: $2,000

- $100K: $3,000

- $150K: $4,500

- If tradable balance falls to $10,000 or below regardless of the acount size, DLL becomes $2,000 (returns to tier DLL once end-of-day balance is back above $10,000)

- Risk violations can trigger a “Shoulder Tap” and traders will be call down to sim-funded

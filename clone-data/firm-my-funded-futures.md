Firm Rules

**News Trading Rules**

- My Funded Futures prohibits exploiting news volatility (e.g., straddles, strangles, or disguised news trades) Tier 1 news has stricter rules: Pro Sim Funded and Rapid Sim Funded accounts cannot trade during T1 events, no open positions/orders 2 minutes before or after T1 data release. while Flex and builder accounts are unrestricted. Traders may trade through non-T1 news only if following their normal strategy, but MFFU is not liable for broker disruptions during news.

**Automated Trading Protocols and EA's**

- Automated strategies are allowed only if they don’t exploit simulator fills, and live-account automation must follow CME guidelines.

**Copy trading and trade copiers**

- Traders may make use of automated trading strategies tailored to their won specific settings so long as these automated tools do not aim to exploit the favorable fills offered in the Simulated Environment.

- Traders trading live accounts with automated trading MUST abide by CME guidelines.


**2% Price Limit Rule**

- Trading is not allowed when a product is within 2% of its CME price limit to protect traders from extreme volatility. Applies to Sim and Live Funded accounts.


See details: [https://help.myfundedfutures.com/en/articles/9698984-2-price-limit-rule](https://help.myfundedfutures.com/en/articles/9698984-2-price-limit-rule)

**Cross Instrument Policy**

- Single Instrument Limit: Orders exceeding the allowed contract size for a single instrument (e.g., more than 2 contracts on NQ) will be automatically rejected.


​
- Cross Instrument Trading: Orders that combine different instruments (e.g., 2 minis and 20 micros) may technically execute even if they exceed the overall limit (e.g., more than 2 contracts in total), but this is not permitted!


​
- Intentional Rule Bypass: Deliberate attempts to circumvent position scaling rules are considered a breach and will be treated accordingly.

**Overnight Holding**

- Not allowed. Trades will automatically close at 4:10 PM / 16:10 EST - It will not result in a breach of the account

**Trading Hours**

- 18:00-16:10 EST (Eastern Standard Time / NY Time)
- 06:00 PM - 04:10 PM EST (Eastern Standard Time / NY Time)

**Inactivity Rule**

- To maintain an active Sim Funded Account, traders must place at least one trade every seven days cycle. Traders planning to take a vacation or a break from trading must inform the support team in advance.

Payout Policy

**Flex Plan**

**25K Account:**

- Qualification for Payouts: 5 winning days of $100

- Withdrawal Limit: Minimum of $250

- Maximum of 50% of total profits up to $3k

- Maximum Loss Limit (MLL): After first payout, MLL moves to $100 and remains static.

- Profit Split: 80/20


**50K account:**

- 5 winning days of $150

- Withdrawal Limit: Minimum of $250

- Maximum of 50% of total profits up to $5k

- Maximum Loss Limit (MLL): After first payout, MLL moves to $100 and remains static.

- Profit Split: 80/20


**Builder Account**

**50K Account:**

- Qualification for Payouts: every 48 hours

- Buffer Requirement:


Your buffer must be fully cleared before you can submit any payout request. The buffer equals your MLL + $100 ($1,600 or $2,100 depending on your MLL selection).

- Net Profit Required (First Payout)


$500 above buffer

- Withdrawal Limit: Minimum of $500

- Maximum Payout: $2000 per cycle

- Profit Split: 80/20


**Pro Accounts**

- Traders are eligible for a payout every 14 days. Min payout is $1000 and Max total payout in the Sim Funded stage is $100,000 per user.

- No Max Payout per Request

- Profit Split: 80/20

- After your first payout, the Max Loss Limit locks to starting balance + $100 (that is, $50,100)


**Pro accounts buffer targets**

- 50k → $2,100

- 100k → $3,100

- 150k →$4,600


**Withdrawal While in Buffer**

- You can withdraw up to 60% of your profits before fully clearing the buffer.

**Account Lifecycle and Live Transition**

**Pro Plan**

- Reviewed for Live Account transition after 3 consecutive payouts, or $20,000 profit milestone

- Risk team may also fast-track based on performance

- Excess profits beyond $100,000 Sim cap may be transitioned to Live (up to plan-specific limits)


**Rapid Plan**

- Profit split: 90%

**Payout requirements**

- Required Buffer:
  - 25K → $1,100
  - 50k → $2,100
  - 100k → $3,100
  - 150k → $4,600
- You must first build the required buffer per account size before requesting any payout.

- Consistency Requirement: None (You don’t need to meet a consistency rule to get paid)


**Timing & Frequency**

- Payout Frequency: Daily

- The payout window refreshes at market close.

- Your first payout becomes available exactly 24 hours after your first trade on the Sim Funded account (as long as you meet the buffer and minimum payout Amount).

- Minimum Payout Request: $500 \| Max total payout in the Sim Funded stage is $100,000 per User \| No Max Payout per Request


**Max Loss Lock at $100**

- Once your trailing Max Loss reaches $100, it locks there. From that point on, it does not trail anymore. You must always keep at least $100 in the account. \| If your balance goes below $100, the account is breached.
